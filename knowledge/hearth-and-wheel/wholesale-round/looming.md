# Definition

**Looming** is the status of a wholesale account whose **standing order is approaching renewal or renegotiation**. For example, a 3-month bread supply agreement that is coming up for renewal is "looming".

# Finding out if a customer is looming

A customer counts as looming if their wholesale account's standing order is approaching renewal or renegotiation. Looming belongs to an **account**, and every looming account sits in exactly one [constellation](/wholesale-round/constellations.md).

# What happens when an account is looming

- It is often flagged for an **in-person visit** during the cafe's next delivery slot on [The Wheel](/wholesale-round/the-wheel.md).
- [Michael](/shop-and-customer-line/michael.md) can tell a cafe partner who calls that their account is looming for renewal.
