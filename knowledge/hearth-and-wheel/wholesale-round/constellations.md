# Definition

**Constellations** are the groups Hearth & Wheel uses to organise its wholesale cafe customers. They are defined **by delivery route**, not by size or order volume.

Each constellation has a name. For example, "Orion" might cover the east-side cafes and "Lyra" the north-loop cafes. A cafe belongs to exactly one constellation.

# How they are used

- [The Wheel](/wholesale-round/the-wheel.md) schedules deliveries **by constellation**, not by individual cafe. To know when a cafe's bread arrives, find its constellation and then look up that constellation on the Wheel.
- A [looming](/wholesale-round/looming.md) renewal always belongs to one account within a specific constellation.
