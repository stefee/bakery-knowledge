# Definition

**The Wheel** is the weekly delivery rotation board for the wholesale bread round. It shows **which [constellations](/wholesale-round/constellations.md) receive bread on which days, and in what delivery order**.

The bakery bakes overnight and delivers wholesale orders each morning on this rotating schedule.

# Answering "is my delivery coming?"

1. Find the cafe's [constellation](/wholesale-round/constellations.md).
2. Look up that constellation on this week's Wheel: which day, and where in the delivery order.
3. Check whether anything disrupted the plan (see below).

# Reshuffles

A poor overnight [Ember Index](/bakehouse/ember-index.md), meaning spoiled or scorched batches, can force that day's Wheel to be **reshuffled**. Affected constellations are delayed or substituted.

# Looming visits

A cafe whose account is [looming](/wholesale-round/looming.md) for renewal is often flagged for an **in-person visit** during their next Wheel delivery slot.
