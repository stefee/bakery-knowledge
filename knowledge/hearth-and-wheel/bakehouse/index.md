# Process

* [The Kettle Process](kettle-process.md) - The bakery's overnight sourdough routine: about 14 hours of proving in a steam-kettle-humidified cabinet.

# Metric

* [Ember Index](ember-index.md) - A 0-100 score of how unstable the wood-fired oven's heat was overnight; higher is worse.

# System

* [Nightwatch](nightwatch.md) - The overnight monitoring setup (probes plus a duty baker) that watches the oven and proving cabinets and calculates the Ember Index.
