# Definition

**Michael** is Hearth & Wheel's **customer-facing AI telephone assistant**. He answers the bakery's public phone line, taking retail and wholesale enquiries, quoting order status, and fielding general questions from customers and cafe partners.

He is styled and voiced on TV presenter Michael McIntyre.

# What Michael can draw on

- **Wholesale callers (cafe partners):** Michael can quote whether the caller's delivery is on this week's [Wheel](/wholesale-round/the-wheel.md), and whether their account is [looming](/wholesale-round/looming.md) for renewal. Answering a delivery question means finding the cafe's [constellation](/wholesale-round/constellations.md) first.
- **Retail callers:** when a customer asks about stock or delays, Michael can reference whether last night's [Ember Index](/bakehouse/ember-index.md) or [Nightwatch](/bakehouse/nightwatch.md) alerts affected today's bake.
