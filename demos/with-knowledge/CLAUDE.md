## Company knowledge

Our internal terminology, processes and systems are documented in the company
knowledge base (`knowledge` MCP server). Your training data doesn't include it.

- Look terms up there before answering or writing code that uses them, including
  ordinary-looking words that may have a special meaning here (as "sprint" or
  "pipeline" do at many companies). When unsure, search.
- If the knowledge base has no answer, say so and ask. Don't guess.
